## Signal Quality Guide
- > -50 dBm: Excellent
- -50 to -60 dBm: Good
- -60 to -70 dBm: Fair (acceptable)
- < -70 dBm: Poor (move node or add repeater)

## Recommendations
- Current install location signal is acceptable
- Keep node within 5m of WiFi router
- Avoid metal scaffolding between node and router
- Monitor RSSI in soak test logs regularly
